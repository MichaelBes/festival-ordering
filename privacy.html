Privacy Policy

St. George Egyptian Festival — Online Ordering

Last updated: July 29, 2026

This page explains what information is collected through the St. George Egyptian Festival's online ordering system, and how it's used.

Information We Collect

  • Your name, used to identify your order for pickup.
  • Your phone number, if you choose to provide one, used only to send you text updates about your order.
  • Details of your order — the items and total — so it can be prepared and picked up correctly.

Payment Information

Card payments are processed securely by Square. We do not collect, see, or store your card details — all payment information is handled directly by Square's own secure systems, separate from this website.

How We Use Your Phone Number

If you provide a phone number, it's used only to send you SMS updates about your own order (for example, confirming it was received, or letting you know it's ready for pickup). Message frequency depends on your order activity — typically one or two messages per order. Message and data rates may apply.

We do not sell, rent, or share your phone number or any personal information with third parties for promotional or marketing purposes.

Opting Out

You can reply STOP to any text message from us at any time to unsubscribe from SMS updates. Reply HELP for assistance. Providing a phone number is optional and is never required to place an order.

Contact

Questions about this policy or how your information is used? Reach out to St. George Coptic Orthodox Church of Illinois, 4601 W Pauling Rd, Monee, IL 60449.
